Thank you always, developers.

###### Reproduction Steps

1. On `Decks` screen, tap a deck which doesn't have any "due" cards* at the moment
*it includes the case that the deck is empty![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163318232-74b2f7cb-06bb-4cbe-9aaf-7bcc75d9724f.png)
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163321666-ccfd4ec9-c0b0-4705-8bca-11df1ea8e47f.png)

2. Press Back button (twice if necessary), or close AnkiDroid completely. Anyway, go back to Android Home screen 
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163318492-8757c6b8-ed40-44c8-9dd2-f4e4afd067f4.png)
3. Long tap (press) AnkiDroid app icon
4. Tap `Study` on the shortcut menu
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163318567-d8968c4b-cb99-4942-8931-e9abdde5cf1c.png)


###### Expected Result
AnkiDroid shows some message which explains the last-selected deck doesn't have any due cards at the moment (or is empty).
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163319862-486c8d1e-7741-4409-895d-8fc5d20e8c73.png)
![image](https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/10436072/163321397-4c97ca21-6760-49f8-aa8e-38038bce48f0.png)



###### Actual Result
AnkiDroid "crashes". I mean, it closes immediately and unexpectedly without any explanation.
