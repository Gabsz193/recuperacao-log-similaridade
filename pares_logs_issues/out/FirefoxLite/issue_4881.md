# 4881 - [Shopping Search] Crash when viewing video in fullscreen

- URL: https://github.com/mozilla-mobile/FirefoxLite/issues/4881
- State: closed
- Author: youwenliang
- Created: 2020-04-09T08:11:57Z

---

**Describe the bug**
When you view video in fullscreen with Shopping search, the app crashes.

**Steps To Reproduce**
Steps to reproduce the behavior:
1. Go to shopping search
2. Search a keyword and see the results
3. In the google tab choose video search
4. Open any video
5. Tap fullscreen button to view the video in fullscreen

**Expected behavior**
Viewing video in fullscreen

**Actual behavior**
App crashes

Version: 2.1.12(19244)

Thanks @proevan for finding this bug.
