# 118 - Deleting a pics in an activity works on another activity.

- URL: https://github.com/ramack/ActivityDiary/issues/118
- State: closed
- Author: mcyusuf
- Created: 2018-03-12T20:36:33Z

---


#### Expected behavior
Pics should be deleted from the activity clicked on.

#### Actual behavior
The pics doesn't delete on that particular activity, the delete works on pics in another activity at the top.

#### How to reproduce
Download app from fdroid.<br>
Open and select an activity, then add a pics to it.<br>
Do the same for another activity.<br>
Open the side menu and go to diary.<br>
Delete the pics attached to the former activity which would be below<br>
Notice the pics doesn't shows it's deleted,
but the latter one goes off as deleted. <br>

* App: Activity diary.     Fdroid store. Version 1.1.8
* Operating system: Android Nougat 7.0 on Tecno K7.
